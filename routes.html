Helpful answers

Route and booking questions

Information to help you contact Star Bus with the right details.

How do I confirm departure times?

Use the WhatsApp booking form or contact Star Bus directly. Ask the team to confirm the current departure for your route and travel date.

Where can I find ticket prices?

Prices can change. Contact Star Bus to confirm the current fare for your chosen route before travelling.

Does the website confirm my ticket automatically?

No. The form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. Your booking is not confirmed until Star Bus replies and confirms the details.
